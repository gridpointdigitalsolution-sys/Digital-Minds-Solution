1. Who We Are

Digital Minds Solutions LLC ("DMS", "we", "us", or "our") is a digital marketing and web development agency headquartered in Houston, Texas, United States. We operate the website at digitalminds.solutions and all subdomains thereof (the "Site").

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: GridpointDigitalSolution@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you provide when you interact with us, including:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, company name, phone number, service interests, budget range, and message content.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: name and email address.
  • Booking requests: information collected when you book a strategy call via Cal.com, including your name, email, and any information you share in the booking form.
  • Client communications: information exchanged during email, WhatsApp, or call communications.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we automatically collect:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Device information: device type, screen resolution, and general location (city/country level) derived from your IP address.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, time spent on pages, links clicked, and navigation paths.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: as described in Section 5 below.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, and LinkedIn Insight Tag when you interact with our advertisements or are referred to our Site from their platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries: Processing and responding to contact form submissions and booking requests.
  • To provide our services: Delivering digital marketing, web design, and related services to clients.
  • To send marketing communications: Sending our weekly insights newsletter to subscribers who opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • To analyse and improve our Site: Understanding how visitors use our Site so we can improve its content, performance, and user experience.
  • To run advertising campaigns: Serving and measuring the performance of digital advertising on Google, Meta, and LinkedIn platforms.
  • To comply with legal obligations: Meeting our obligations under applicable law, including retaining business records.
  • To protect our rights: Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, security breaches, and other potentially prohibited or illegal activities.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Ever.

4.3 Business Transfers

If Digital Minds Solutions LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal information may be transferred. We will provide notice before your personal information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyse Site usage and serve relevant advertising. The types of cookies we use are:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the Site to function. These cannot be opted out of.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our Site. These use anonymised IP addresses and do not personally identify you.
  • Advertising cookies: Google Ads, Meta Pixel, and LinkedIn Insight Tag cookies used to measure ad performance and serve relevant advertising to people who have visited our Site.
  • Preference cookies: Cookies that remember your preferences such as your chosen colour theme (dark/light mode) and language setting.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your experience on our Site. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Enquiry and contact data: Retained for up to 3 years after your last interaction with us.
  • Newsletter subscriber data: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Client records: Retained for 7 years after the end of the business relationship in accordance with US tax and accounting requirements.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics data is retained for 26 months by default.

When personal information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of marketing: Opt out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or contacting us directly.
  • Right to data portability: Request your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at GridpointDigitalSolution@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 45 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) provides you with the following additional rights:

8.1 Categories of Personal Information Collected

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email, IP address); commercial information (services enquired about, budget ranges); internet or other electronic network activity (browsing behaviour on our Site); geolocation data (city/country level only); and inferences drawn from the above.

8.2 No Sale of Personal Information

Digital Minds Solutions LLC does not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA. We do share certain identifiers with advertising platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) for targeted advertising purposes. You may opt out of this sharing by contacting us.

8.3 Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights. We will not deny you goods or services, charge you different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

8.4 How to Submit a Request

California residents may submit a verifiable consumer request by emailing GridpointDigitalSolution@gmail.com with the subject line "CCPA Privacy Request". We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days, with a possible 45-day extension if needed.

9. EU / UK Data Subjects (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the following additional provisions apply to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.

9.1 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: Operating our business, responding to enquiries, and analysing Site usage.
  • Consent: Sending marketing emails and using non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: Processing data necessary to deliver services to clients.
  • Legal obligation: Retaining business records as required by law.

Digital Minds Solutions LLC is based in the United States. If you are located in the EEA or UK, your personal data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other appropriate safeguards where applicable for such transfers.

9.3 Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you are an EU or UK resident, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe we have processed your personal data unlawfully.
