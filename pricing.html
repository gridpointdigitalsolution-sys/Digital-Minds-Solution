Common questions

Everything you need to know before booking

If you have a question that isn't answered here, book a free 30-minute call and we'll walk you through exactly what to expect.

How do I know which tier is right for my business?
The right tier depends on where you are and where you need to go. If you have one specific problem to fix, Optimise is the right move. If you need to build or rebuild a multi-channel growth system, Grow or Transform is the fit. Book a free call and we'll scope it together in 30 minutes. no obligation, no pitch deck.
Are the prices shown the exact amount I'll pay?
The ranges shown are starting points based on typical project scopes. Your final investment is fixed at the proposal stage and locked in a written contract before any work begins. We do not bill by the hour and we do not increase scope without written agreement. What you sign is what you pay.
How does payment work?
For projects up to $10,000 we typically split 50% on contract signing and 50% on delivery. For larger projects we use 3-stage milestone billing. kick-off, mid-point, and delivery. so your cash outflow is tied to real progress, not calendar dates. We accept ACH, wire transfer, and all major cards.
Do you work with businesses outside the United States?
Yes. We're a Houston-based team but we have delivered projects across 9 countries including the UK, UAE, France, Spain, Nigeria, and Australia. Time zone differences are managed through async-first communication with scheduled weekly check-ins that work for your hours.
Who actually does the work?
Every deliverable is produced in-house by our Houston team. We do not outsource to freelancers, subcontract offshore, or white-label any work. Churchill and Phillip are directly involved in every engagement. not as relationship managers, but as practitioners producing the actual work.
How long does a typical project take?
Optimise projects complete in 2–4 weeks. Grow projects run 4–8 weeks. Transform projects are 8–14 weeks from kick-off to go-live. Build timelines are scoped individually based on complexity. We are known for being faster than the industry average. our landing page builds go live in 5 business days and our CRM setups complete in 3 weeks.
What happens after the project is delivered?
All projects include a post-delivery support window (30–90 days depending on tier) for bug fixes and implementation questions. Many clients move into a monthly retainer for ongoing SEO, paid media management, or content production after the initial project delivers results.
Do you offer retainer engagements?
Yes. After a successful project, clients can move to a monthly retainer for continuous improvement across SEO, paid media, email, and content. Retainers start at $2,500/month and are scoped to a specific outcome. not a list of activities. You're paying for results, not hours.
