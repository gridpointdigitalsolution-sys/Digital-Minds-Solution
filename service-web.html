FAQ

Questions we always
get asked.

Honest answers. No marketing fluff.

How long does a website build take?+
A standard marketing website takes 4–6 weeks. A full ecommerce store or web app takes 8–14 weeks. We don't cut corners to hit artificial deadlines.
Do you build on WordPress or custom?+
Both. We recommend the right tool for your business. WordPress for content-heavy sites. Custom builds for complex functionality. Webflow for marketing teams who need design control.
Will my site rank on Google?+
Every site we build includes on-page SEO, structured data, sitemap submission, and Core Web Vitals optimisation. It isn't an add-on. It's the foundation.
Do you redesign existing sites?+
Yes. We audit your current site, identify what's working and what isn't, then rebuild with your audience and conversion goals as the brief. Not just aesthetics.
What happens after launch?+
We monitor for 30 days post-launch, then run an optimisation sprint based on real user behaviour. Ongoing retainers are available for continuous improvement.
